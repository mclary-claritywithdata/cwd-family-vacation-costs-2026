# What Does a Family Vacation Cost in 2026?

## Clarity With Data Analytics Project

### Business Question

How much does a typical family vacation cost in 2026, and how has that changed over time?

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes family vacation costs from 2020 through 2026 to understand how travel expenses have evolved and identify the primary drivers of vacation inflation.

The analysis focuses on:

- Airfare
- Lodging
- Food & Dining
- Transportation
- Entertainment
- Total Trip Cost

---

## Objectives

- Measure vacation cost inflation over time
- Identify the largest contributors to rising travel costs
- Compare category-level spending trends
- Build an interactive dashboard for consumers and planners
- Demonstrate end-to-end analytics workflow
